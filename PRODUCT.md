## Platform

web

## Users

Residential property investors in South East Queensland who own one to a handful of long-term rental properties in Brisbane, the Gold Coast, or the Sunshine Coast. Many are mid-career professionals, often living elsewhere (interstate, overseas, or across town), who bought a rental as a long-term wealth play and do not want to run it themselves. The second group is owners already with an agency who are unhappy with slow replies, missed maintenance, or long vacancies and are looking to switch. They arrive from search ("property management Brisbane"), a referral, or a portal, and they are comparing agencies.

Tenants are a secondary audience: they reach the site for support contact and listings, but the owner is the one who signs the management agreement.

## Product Purpose

Homefront Committed Property Management manages long-term residential rentals on behalf of owners: finding and screening tenants, collecting rent, coordinating maintenance, running routine inspections, and giving tenants 24/7 support. The website exists to turn a comparing owner into a request for a free rental appraisal (the "rental report"), which opens the management conversation. Success is appraisal requests and switch enquiries from owners in the three service regions.

## Positioning

A young, owner-led agency whose whole pitch is attention: every property is treated as if it were the team's own. Against large franchise agencies where one property manager carries hundreds of doors, Homefront's claim is responsiveness and care from people who pick up the phone.

## Operating Context

Owners evaluate the agency on a laptop or phone, usually after hours, often comparing three or four agencies side by side. The decision artefacts are the rental appraisal (expected weekly rent), the management fee, and the owner's sense of who will actually look after the property. After signing, the relationship runs on inspection reports, rent statements, maintenance approvals, and phone calls.

## Product Principles

1. **Attention is the product.** Every surface should make it believable that a person, not a portfolio system, is looking after this house.
2. **Show the work, not the adjectives.** Inspection reports, leased homes, and response times prove care; "meticulous" and "dedicated" do not.
3. **Plain speaking.** Owners are investors, not insiders. Say rent, fees, and next steps in plain terms.
4. **One clear next step.** Every page leads to the rental appraisal without pressure.
