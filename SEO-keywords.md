# Homefront: target keywords (long-term property management, Brisbane + Gold Coast)

Data: Ubersuggest, Australia, pulled 30 Sep 2026. Volumes are monthly estimates and under-count long-tail and suburb terms; treat low numbers as "real but small", not zero. SD = SEO difficulty (0-100). CPC = what agencies pay Google per click, a good proxy for how valuable the lead is.

## Where Homefront stands today

- Ranks only for its own name: "homefront property management" (#1), "homefront real estate" (#3).
- "property management brisbane": position 63. No Gold Coast rankings at all.
- The Google map pack (top 3 local listings) sits above the organic results for every head term. PMC, LINK Living and The Property Collective hold it for "property managers brisbane".
- Ranking organic competitors are small agencies with modest authority (Harcourts Solutions DA 30, Image Property 33, Worth 23, Locate 14, Housemark 17). This market is winnable.

## Deliberately excluded

Airbnb, short-term, holiday and commercial property management terms. They show up in the data but are not Homefront's service, and pages for them would attract the wrong owners.

## Suggested site structure

1. `/property-management-brisbane/` (Tier 1 Brisbane + northside/south sections)
2. `/property-management-gold-coast/` (Tier 1 Gold Coast + Southport and nearby suburbs)
3. `/rental-appraisal/` (rental appraisal, free/online appraisal, appraisal letter, Brisbane and Gold Coast variants)
4. `/fees/` (published fees, QLD averages, a calculator, "what's included")
5. `/switch-property-manager/` (how to change property managers in QLD)
6. Later: suburb pages only where Homefront genuinely manages homes (North Lakes, Southport, etc.), each with real leased examples, not templated copy. Keep it under ~15 to avoid thin, duplicate pages.

## Non-keyword actions that matter more than any page

- **Google Business Profile.** The map pack takes the top of every head-term result. Set up or verify the profile with category "Property management company", service areas Brisbane and Gold Coast, photos, and a steady flow of owner reviews.
- **Title tags.** The homepage title is "Homefront Property Management – We put your property first". Lead with the keyword: "Property Management Brisbane & Gold Coast | Homefront".
- **Reviews on RateMyAgent.** It ranks on page 1 for "property managers brisbane"; a strong Homefront profile there is a second route onto that page.
- **Schema.** Add `RealEstateAgent` LocalBusiness markup with address, phone, areaServed.
